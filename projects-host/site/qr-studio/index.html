    READY FOR THE SLICER

    Aligned parts. Separate colours.

    Backing, QR and lettering stay aligned. Matching colours share a filament slot; a cross blade prints as its own piece.

    3MF includes named parts and Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer filament assignments. Choose your printer profile and check spool mapping in the slicer. Other slicers may require manual colour assignment. STL has no colour metadata.

    Printing & scanning tips

    Print flat, with the QR and text facing up. Use opaque pale filament for the backing and dark filament for the QR. Inspect thin lettering in the sliced preview and scan the finished print before putting it in a pot.

    For STL parts, import 01 Backing, 02 QR and 03 Lettering together as one object with parts. Keep their coordinates; do not arrange or drop raised parts to the bed separately. Import 04 Cross blade separately if present. The fit-test STL is a separate trial print.

    The combined/print-layout STL has no colours. A manual colour change at backing height also colours any thicker spike above that height; use 3MF for independent colours. The default 2.4 mm backing and 0.6 mm details suit 0.2 mm layers. A QR code linking to Plant Manager still uses its normal sign-in.

    Print flat. Slide together.

    1. Download the fit test and print its two small pieces at the blade thickness.
    2. Turn one piece 90° and slide the open slots toward each other. Adjust clearance if the fit is too tight or loose.
    3. Print the tag and its separate cross blade flat. Keep QR and lettering aligned on the tag.
    4. Slide the cross blade upward from the pointed end of the tag’s spike until the slot ends meet. The top of the second blade sits below the flared neck.

    A sliding joint is not a latch. Check the fit before inserting into soil. The assembled view is for inspection; export always lays both pieces flat.
